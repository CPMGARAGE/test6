THE STORY OF TERMINALTANKS

THE FIRST CRISIS

Long before the events of TerminalTanks, the world had already faced a crisis like this one.

Resources were running out. As supplies dwindled, people began fighting over what remained. The most powerful among them became obsessed with what they could take and keep. They gathered enormous reserves, built armies around their wealth, and refused to let anyone else have what they had collected.

They became known as the Ancient Hoarders.

No army could defeat them. The world was running out of time.

Then one person decided to fight back.

A powerful mage gathered the last great reserves of magic and created a spell capable of imprisoning the Hoarders. But the spell required more power than the mage could survive giving.

With no other way to save the world, the mage sacrificed nearly everything to complete the spell.

The Ancient Hoarders were sealed away.

The world survived.

The mage was believed to have died.

The prisons became known as Terminal Tickets.

THE MAGE'S LEGACY

The mage's story eventually became legend. But their bloodline survived.

The mage's descendants carried on for generations, eventually forgetting the true history of their ancestor.

Some of the mage's possessions survived with them. Among them were old tanks and ammunition taken during the First Crisis.

They were never treated as legendary weapons. By the time they reached the player's generation, they were little more than old, rusty family heirlooms.

The first five tanks and five basic ammunition types available to the player are remnants of that ancient war. The player does not know their true origin. They simply inherit them and begin their journey.

They have no idea that the equipment they are carrying belonged to the very person who once saved the world.

THE WORLD FORGOT

Centuries passed.

The story of the Ancient Hoarders faded into myth. The mage became a distant figure from an almost forgotten age. The Terminal Tickets became mysterious artifacts whose original purpose was lost.

Almost nobody remembered that something was still alive inside them.

And nobody knew that the mage's bloodline had survived.

History was about to repeat itself.

THE SECOND RESOURCE CRISIS

Resources became scarce again. People began fighting. Greed returned.

The richest people alive began hoarding what remained, building armies, fortresses, and machines around their wealth. These new hoarders became the bosses of a new age.

The greatest among them was the Master.

The Master controlled more resources than anyone else and built the Master's Citadel to protect them.

The world once again needed someone willing to fight back.

That person was you.

THE ROAD TO THE MASTER

The Tournament became your path through the new generation of hoarders.

Twenty battles. Twenty steps toward the Master's Citadel.

You fought through increasingly powerful opponents, reclaimed their resources, and grew stronger with every victory.

Eventually, you reached the Master.

You defeated him.

The greatest remaining stockpile of resources in the world was no longer controlled by one person. The wealth was reclaimed. The world had a chance to recover.

But your victory had been noticed.

Not only by the people of your world.

THE ALIENS

Another world was running out of resources too.

Its people learned about the enormous wealth that had once belonged to the Master. They came looking for it.

But they arrived too late.

The Master had already fallen. The riches were already in your hands.

Their target changed.

You became the target.

THE INVASION

The alien invasion began.

What started with individual UFO encounters escalated into a full-scale assault.

You fought increasingly powerful alien forces. The UFOs became stronger. The battles became harder.

Eventually, the Archon appeared.

And then came the greatest attack yet.

The Mothership arrived with two powerful drones.

The invasion had reached its final stage.

You fought through the assault and defeated the Mothership.

The invasion ended.

The resources reclaimed from the hoarders could finally be protected and returned to the people.

For the first time in generations, the world had peace.

Or so it seemed.

THE TERMINAL TICKETS

The oldest threat had never been destroyed.

The Terminal Tickets were not treasures.

They were prisons.

The ten most powerful Ancient Hoarders from the First Crisis had never been defeated. The mage had been unable to destroy them. Instead, they were bound inside magical prisons and sealed away.

The mage gave everything to create those seals.

For generations, they remained hidden.

Until now.

The Terminal Tickets were rediscovered.

When a Ticket was activated, its seal was broken and the imprisoned Hoarder was released.

Each ancient enemy had their own tank, battlefield, ammunition, and fighting style.

These were not ordinary bosses.

They were the enemies the ancient mage had only been able to contain.

Now you had the chance to finish what they started.

THE TEN HOARDERS

One by one, you defeated them.

Each victory permanently ended another threat from the First Crisis.

There were ten.

When the final Ancient Hoarder fell, the Terminal Tickets were exhausted.

The oldest enemies in history were finally gone.

You had finished the war that your ancestor could only imprison.

But there was one final secret.

THE MARTYR'S ARCANE TIME MACHINE

The story of the mage did not end with the First Crisis.

After the final Terminal Ticket was defeated, a final artifact became available: the Martyr's Arcane Time Machine.

It was the most powerful and expensive artifact ever discovered.

Its true purpose was unknown.

Until it was activated.

THE TRUTH

The mage had never truly died.

The magic required to imprison the Ancient Hoarders had destroyed the mage's mortal body. But at the final moment, the mage found another way.

Using an artifact capable of existing outside the normal flow of time, the mage sealed their consciousness, soul, and remaining arcane power within it.

The mage survived.

But not as an ordinary human.

For centuries, they existed outside normal time, bound to the artifact and sustained by the power that remained within them.

The mage had become something between human, spirit, and living arcane force.

The world believed the mage had died.

The mage knew otherwise.

THE DESCENDANT

The mage recognized you immediately.

You were not simply another warrior.

You were their descendant.

Their many-greats-grandchild.

The bloodline had survived.

And so had pieces of the mage's past.

The old tanks and ammunition you had started your journey with were not random beginner equipment.

They were relics of the First Crisis.

They had once belonged to your ancestor. They had been passed from generation to generation until their history was almost completely forgotten.

The very equipment that began your journey had come from the war that began the story.

The mage had watched your journey from outside normal time.

They had seen the Master fall.

They had seen the alien invasion.

They had seen the Mothership destroyed.

They had seen the Ancient Hoarders released from their prisons.

And they had seen every one of them defeated.

The mage tells you that you have fought well.

You have saved the world.

You have protected its people.

You have reclaimed what was stolen.

You have ended threats that existed long before your birth.

You have accomplished what generations before you could not.

But there is one final question.

THE MARTYR'S DUEL

The mage challenges you.

Not because they have become evil.

Not because they want to conquer the world.

But because there is one thing they need to know.

Has their descendant surpassed them?

Ancestor against descendant.

Mage against heir.

The one who began the fight against the one who finished it.

The final battle begins.

PHASE I — THE ANCIENT BATTLEFIELD

The first battlefield is a place from the age of the First Crisis.

Ancient ruins surround the arena. The remains of old fortifications and forgotten machines sit beneath the battlefield.

This is where the mage once fought.

The Mage begins by testing their descendant.

Their power is unlike anything encountered before.

They wield the elements themselves:

Fire. Water. Earth. Wind. Light. Darkness.

Each element possesses its own attacks and battlefield effects.

PHASE II — THE COLLAPSE OF TIME

The Mage begins releasing more of their power.

The battlefield starts to break apart. Time itself begins to distort.

Fragments of different eras appear within the arena: ancient battlefields, modern structures, alien technology, and remnants of the Terminal Tickets.

Different elements begin combining. The environment becomes increasingly dangerous.

The fight is no longer taking place in a normal battlefield.

It is taking place outside normal time.

PHASE III — THE FINAL AGE

The final battlefield is unlike anything that has existed before.

Reality itself seems to have opened.

Fragments of the world's history surround the battlefield: ancient ruins, modern technology, alien machinery, arcane energy, broken remnants of the Terminal Tickets, and pieces of battlefields from across your journey.

Everything that came before has converged into one final arena.

The Mage releases their full power.

Fire consumes the battlefield. Water crashes through the terrain. Wind tears across the arena. Earth rises and collapses. Light cuts through the darkness. Darkness consumes what remains.

The battlefield itself becomes part of the fight.

This is no longer a battle between ordinary tanks.

It is the final confrontation between two generations.

The Mage, the legend who saved the world during the First Crisis, and their descendant, the warrior who ended everything that came after.

Both combatants possess enormous reserves of health. Special attacks are unleashed. Ancient ammunition and modern weapons collide.

Neither side gives way.

Until finally...

The Mage falls.

THE END OF THE LEGACY

The Mage's power begins to fade.

The artifact that preserved them for centuries finally reaches its end.

But there is no anger.

Only peace.

The Mage has finally seen the answer to the question that brought them into the final battle.

Their descendant has surpassed them.

The work they began during the First Crisis has finally been completed.

The Ancient Hoarders are gone.

The Master is gone.

The alien invasion is over.

The Mothership has fallen.

The Terminal Tickets are empty.

The world is safe.

The Mage's sacrifice was not forgotten.

Their legacy survived.

Not because their descendant became another version of them.

But because their descendant became something greater.

The Mage's story finally comes to an end.

THE ANCIENT ARTIFACTS

After the final battle, the player inherits the ultimate remnants of the First Crisis.

Five Ancient Artifact Tanks.

Five Ancient Artifact Ammunition types.

These artifacts stand apart from everything created afterward.

Their designs come from an age before modern TerminalTanks technology. Their components, effects, animations, and visual language are unlike ordinary equipment.

They are not merely powerful.

They are pieces of history.

Three final Main Screens commemorate the three battlefields of the Mage's final duel. Each preserves the memory of one stage of the final confrontation.

THE FINAL SCREEN

The wars are over.

The world is safe.

The legacy has been completed.

The final Main Screen does not celebrate with a declaration of victory.

It simply remains.

A quiet reminder of everything that had to happen to reach this point.

The player can return whenever they want.

The equipment remains.

The memories remain.

The world remains.

And somewhere in the silence is the weight of everything that happened.

The Mage's story began with sacrifice.

The player's story began with five old tanks and five forgotten weapons.

And it ended with the descendant finally finishing what the Mage started.

HOW IT IS BUILT

TerminalTanks is one Python file of about 10,000 lines. It needs Python 3.10 or newer and a UTF-8 terminal of at least 96 by 28 characters. It uses no third-party packages: drawing, input and animation are all built on the standard library and plain ANSI escape sequences.

RenderingANSI escape sequences, no curses MultiplayerLAN play over TCP, with UDP discovery so hosts appear on their own FairnessThe simulation is seeded and deterministic, and visual effects use their own random generator so they cannot change an outcome SavesOne small JSON file, versioned, with migration for older saves and room for fields from newer ones In the browserThe same game file on the Pyodide Python runtime, drawn by a hand-written canvas renderer

The deterministic design is what makes LAN play work: both machines run the same simulation from the same starting setup, so a shot only needs to send its angle and power. It also keeps matches reproducible.

HOW THE BROWSER VERSION WORKS

The browser version runs the same terminaltanks.py as the terminal, but not literally untouched. The page supplies stand-ins for the operating-system terminal modules the game imports and paces the game one frame at a time. Scenes, physics, AI and saves are the same code in both places.

The page draws the game with its own canvas renderer, with no terminal library and no other third-party code. The one outside piece is Pyodide, the Python runtime itself, which downloads on your first visit and is cached after that. The game and its renderer still follow the project's rule of no dependencies of their own.

Saves have changed shape over time, and the game still reads older versions and converts them. Custom tanks are stored by stable part names rather than positions in a list, so a tank you build today still loads after new parts are added, and a part that is ever removed falls back to a safe default instead of breaking the save.

TANKS OF YOUR OWN

The Workshop lets you assemble a tank from parts you have unlocked: a turret, hull, tracks, barrel, glow, ambient effect and accent color. Name it, give it a line of lore, preview it, and equip it. Saving a design costs coins, and you can keep up to of them. Official tanks are never overwritten.
